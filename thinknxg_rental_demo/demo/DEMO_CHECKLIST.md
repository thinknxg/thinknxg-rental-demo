# Presenter checklist

## 1. Start in the yard

- Open **Rental Item Profile** and search for **NXGRD-FW-1200**. Show the daily, weekly and monthly rates and the agreed repair, loss and beyond-repair amounts.
- Open the submitted **Stock Reconciliation** from the loader output. The 12 lines add up to **2,062 units** and **AED 131,380.00** opening stock value.
- Use **Rental Inventory** and **Material at Site** to compare owned and cross-hire ownership. For historical report filters, use a date on or after the final stock movement printed by the loader.

## 2. Explain the owned-stock billing

- The first seven days have 60 panels, 80 props and 120 clamps on hire. Daily rates are AED 3.50, AED 1.20 and AED 0.35 respectively. The first seven days total AED 2,436.00.
- The next seven days have 39 panels, 60 props and 80 clamps on hire and total AED 1,655.50. The returned and lost quantities stop billing after the off-hire date because the physical return falls within the one-day grace period.
- Open the draft recovery invoice. The agreement charges two repairable panels at AED 35.00, one beyond repair at AED 180.00 and one lost at AED 220.00.
- Open the draft **Rental Pro Forma Invoice**. The originally planned hire is AED 9,744.00 before tax and its refundable deposit is AED 2,200.00. Open the period billing invoice separately to show the effect of actual movements. With a standard 5% rental rule and an out-of-scope deposit, the draft pro forma shows AED 487.20 VAT and AED 12,431.20 total payable before collections.

## 3. Walk the job and cross-hire documents

- In **Job Completion Report**, use the erection and dismantle dates to explain the included period and excess days. Compare the JCR Billing Schedule lines with the draft invoices.
- In the cross-hire **Purchase Order**, compare the zero-rate equipment line with the non-stock hire charge line. Open the Purchase Receipt and supplier Purchase Return to show the zero-value custody movements. Open the draft supplier Purchase Invoice to show the hire expense.
- Return to **Material at Site**. The owned and mixed hire projects together have **191 units** still at site. The job-type kit has been returned in full.

## 4. Optional live steps on the demo site

- To demonstrate receipts and advances, use the pro forma on the Palm Quay contract. Review the UAE Rental Company Profile and liability accounts, approve the pro forma, then create a **Deposit Collection** for part of the AED 2,200 deposit. Open the generated receipt and Journal Entry. To demonstrate conversion separately from movement billing, create a pro forma for another hire and convert it after acceptance.
- To demonstrate the customer portal, use the staff preview at `/rental` and choose the Palm Quay customer. For a customer login test, link a test user under that customer's Portal Users. The package creates no users or credentials.
- To demonstrate closing the supplier order, finish reviewing and submit its supplier bill, then use **Complete Order** on the Cross Hire Order.
